# Roam Beyond Privacy Policy Last updated: October 2026 Roam Beyond respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Roam Beyond collects, uses and protects information when you use the application. ## Information We Collect Roam Beyond may collect: - Email address for account creation and sign in - User profile information - Destinations, posts and comments submitted by users - Saved destinations and visited destinations - Device identifiers used to support anonymous contributions - Location information when explicitly provided or selected by the user ## How We Use Information We use information to: - Provide app functionality - Display destinations, posts and community contributions - Maintain user accounts - Improve app performance and reliability - Detect abuse, spam and inappropriate content ## Data Storage Data is stored securely using third-party infrastructure providers including Supabase. ## Contact For privacy questions contact: support@roambeyond.app
